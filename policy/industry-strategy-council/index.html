Contributing an industry perspective on recovery.

Participated in the digital-industries stakeholder consultations documented in Canada’s economic recovery report.

Industry Strategy Council Consultation

Focus of the work

The Industry Strategy Council’s ‘Restart, Recover and Reimagine Prosperity for All Canadians’ report lists me among the digital-industries stakeholders engaged during its consultations.

This was a contribution to the consultation process, distinct from membership of the Industry Strategy Council itself. The report brought industry perspectives into the discussion of economic recovery and Canada’s longer-term competitiveness.
