G7 Multistakeholder Conference on AI

Focus of the work

  • Multistakeholder dialogue
  • Responsible AI
  • The social and economic context of adoption

At the G7 Multistakeholder Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Montréal, I moderated the opening ‘Setting the Stage’ discussion with Verity Harding, Fanny Hidvégi, Edward Santow and Stefan Schnorr.

The conference brought government, business, research and civil-society voices together around responsible AI and its wider social and economic implications. The government’s final summary documents the session and its participants.
