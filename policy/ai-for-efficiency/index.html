Recognizing useful applications of AI.

Contributed to the jury for the French Ministry of the Economy’s AI for Efficiency initiative around the AI Action Summit.

France · AI for Efficiency

Focus of the work

  • Applied AI
  • Organizational usefulness
  • Innovation evaluation

I contributed to the jury for AI for Efficiency, an initiative associated with the 2025 AI Action Summit and the French Ministry of the Economy.

The programme highlighted applications of AI that improve the way organizations and services operate. It connects with a central interest of mine: judging technology by the practical value it creates.
