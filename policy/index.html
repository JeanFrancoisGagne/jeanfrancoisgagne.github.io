Policy & public service

Make AI useful.
Make it worth trusting.

My policy work starts from the same place as my product work: people should have a meaningful say in the systems that affect them.

Principles need a path
into practice.

I have worked with governments and international groups on trustworthy AI, commercialization, digital public services and regulation.

The focus is practical: connect values to design choices, clarify responsibility, and give organizations a way to assess what they are deploying.
