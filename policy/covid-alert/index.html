Policy & public service/Canada’s COVID Alert Advisory Council

2020–2021 / Co-Chair

Trust is part of how a public service works.

Co-chaired the council advising on inclusion, adoption, privacy and the operation of Canada’s exposure-notification service.

Canada’s COVID Alert Advisory Council

Focus of the work

  • Inclusion, accessibility and public trust
  • Privacy-preserving evaluation
  • Coordination and service delivery

I co-chaired Canada’s COVID Alert Advisory Council with Carole Piovesan. The council brought together different perspectives on a difficult public-service question: how a privacy-preserving digital tool could be useful, accessible and trusted during a public-health emergency.

The council’s three reports addressed adoption and retention; COVID Alert as a government service; and the tool’s public-health contribution and limitations. Our work considered language and access barriers, outreach, the process for issuing notification keys, operational improvements and privacy-preserving measurement.

The council held its nineteenth and final meeting on 29 July 2021. The final public report includes later evaluation data and acknowledges that the app did not reach its full potential. That record matters: responsible adoption includes understanding what worked, what did not, and what future services should learn.
