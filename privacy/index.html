Privacy

A simple, respectful website.

This site is designed to be read without an account.

Audience measurement, with your permission

Google Analytics helps us understand readership across the English and French versions: pages viewed, visits, engagement and the sites that bring readers here. It loads only after you select “Accept”. Declining does not affect access to any content.

Analytics uses cookies with random identifiers and processes technical information such as browser, device and approximate location. Our setup disables advertising personalization and Google signals. We do not send your email address, site-search text, URL query parameters or fragments to Analytics. Referring websites are recorded by domain, without their page paths or query strings.

With your consent, enhanced measurement also records scrolling, clicks on external links, file-download clicks and playback of supported embedded videos. It does not record form interactions or your searches on this site.

Your choice and storage

Your browser remembers your choice for 180 days. You can change it at any time using “Privacy preferences” in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops future measurement and removes this site’s Analytics cookies; it does not erase data already processed. Analytics cookies expire after 90 days and are not extended on each visit. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page.

Hosting providers may process standard request logs to deliver and secure the site, independently of your Analytics choice. Google processes Analytics data on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, publishers and video services open their websites, which have their own privacy practices. The historical research reports include optional Tableau and Flourish charts. They load from those providers only after you choose “Load original chart.”

Contact

Email sent to the contact address is handled through Google Workspace. Messages sent through LinkedIn are handled by LinkedIn. This site does not collect or store contact-form submissions. No mailing-list signup is currently active.
