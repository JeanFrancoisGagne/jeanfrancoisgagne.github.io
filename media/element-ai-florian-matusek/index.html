Building Element AI: a conversation with Florian Matusek

What does it take to build a company around AI research? I joined Florian Matusek for a conversation about Element AI, the people behind it, and the choices ahead for the field.

About this conversation

Recorded after Element AI’s acquisition by ServiceNow, this conversation looks back at building the company and working with Yoshua Bengio. It also considers AI’s opportunities, risks and regulation.

Topics in the conversation

  • The origins of Element AI
  • Working with Yoshua Bengio
  • AI’s opportunities, risks and regulation

Context at the time

The publisher’s description refers to the EU AI Act. My contribution was to the European Commission’s earlier High-Level Expert Group on AI and its trustworthy-AI framework; the legislative text was a separate process.
