DeepSeek and the economics of AI

DeepSeek’s arrival raised a question for the whole AI industry: what changes when capable models become less expensive to develop? I discussed that shift on Radio-Canada’s Zone économie.

About this conversation

This French-language interview responds to DeepSeek’s emergence in January 2025. The discussion concerns the cost of developing AI and how new approaches can spread across the industry.

Topics in the conversation

  • The arrival of DeepSeek
  • The cost of developing AI
  • How new approaches spread across the industry

Context at the time

This interview reflects the developments and information available in January 2025.
