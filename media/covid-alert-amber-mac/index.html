COVID Alert: public trust and privacy

Public trust matters when technology becomes a public service. In October 2020, I joined Amber Mac and Justin Trudeau to discuss Canada’s COVID Alert app.

About this conversation

Amber Mac hosted this conversation with then Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and me in my role as co-chair of the COVID Alert Advisory Council. We discussed the app’s privacy protections, how it worked and where it was available at the time.

Topics in the conversation

  • Privacy and public trust
  • How exposure notifications worked
  • Access to a digital public service

Context at the time

This is a historical conversation from October 2020. References to downloading the app and its availability describe that period, rather than current advice.
