Planora / Omega Optimisation

Product leadership; President & CEO

2007–2012

The work

The problem was concrete: how to make better decisions when demand, staffing needs, constraints and costs interact. I worked across product and company leadership as we developed optimization software for those challenges.

Planora was acquired by RedPrairie in July 2012. The team’s work continued through a Montréal center of excellence and later through JDA.

My contribution

  • Led product development and company direction.
  • Applied optimization to workforce-management decisions.
  • Helped build the company through its acquisition by RedPrairie.
