Real Ventures

Entrepreneur in Residence

2015–2016

The work

That period gave me the space to work with founders, investors and collaborators while the idea that became Element AI took shape. It connected my experience in enterprise software with the advances emerging from AI research.

Mentoring founders has also been a recurring part of my work, including through FounderFuel.

My contribution

  • Explored new company ideas and the application of AI in enterprise software.
  • Developed the relationships and early thinking that led to Element AI.
  • Worked with founders and the Montréal startup community.
