Career/CIMA+

Enterprise adoption / November 2022–May 2024

Making AI adoption practical in engineering.

Through Corporation JFKR, I advised CIMA+ on AI and digital transformation, connecting a firm-wide strategy to the work of its engineering teams.

CIMA+

Strategic Advisor, AI & Digital Transformation

November 2022–May 2024

The work

The mandate covered the opportunities AI creates for an engineering services business, the capabilities needed to pursue them, and how to move from isolated experiments to a coordinated portfolio of initiatives.

My work included AI strategy, the evaluation of opportunities across operating sectors, and the adoption of generative AI tools. The focus was on useful applications and the organizational foundations needed to support them.

My contribution

  • Developed the AI and emerging-technology strategy with senior leadership.
  • Helped teams evaluate and prioritize opportunities across the business.
  • Supported generative AI adoption and the structures needed to put it into practice.
