Career

Building companies.
Connecting ideas to practice.

My work spans entrepreneurship, enterprise software, AI product strategy and public service. The constant is turning technical possibility into something people can use.

Research is a beginning.
Usefulness is the test.

I co-founded and led Element AI, then led AI strategy and product management at ServiceNow. Before that, I worked on optimization at Planora and led product and innovation at JDA.

Today, I’m building Nera: a way for software teams to keep human intent present as AI takes on more of the work.

Alongside building companies, I have contributed to AI policy in Europe and Canada, mentored founders, and written about talent, governance and human agency.
