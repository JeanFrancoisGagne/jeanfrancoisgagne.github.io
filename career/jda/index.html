Product leadership / 2013–2015

Connecting a global product portfolio to its next generation.

JDA Software & JDA Labs

Chief Product Officer; Chief Innovation Officer

2013–2015

The work

As Chief Product Officer, my responsibilities included product strategy, the roadmap and release priorities across a broad portfolio of supply-chain, retail and workforce software. That work required balancing near-term customer needs with the longer-term direction of the business.

As Chief Innovation Officer and head of JDA Labs, I brought together data and decision science, user experience and product innovation. The lab connected research and experimentation to the products and operational problems the company understood best.

My contribution

  • Led strategy and priorities across 40+ product lines.
  • Worked across a global product and engineering organization.
  • Founded JDA Labs and built a multidisciplinary research and product innovation team.
